OUR STORY

Built Around Technology & Trust

TechTray Holdings was founded with a simple goal: make premium technology products more accessible to customers across South Africa.


We carefully source quality electronics, mobile accessories and everyday tech essentials that offer excellent performance and value. From wireless earbuds and fast chargers to smart accessories and networking equipment, every product is selected with reliability in mind.


As our business grows, we remain committed to providing secure shopping, outstanding customer service and dependable nationwide delivery. Our customers are at the heart of everything we do, and we're proud to help individuals, families and businesses stay connected with technology they can trust.
